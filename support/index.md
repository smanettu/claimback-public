# ClaimBack Support

For help, feedback, privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact:

**[smanettu@gmail.com](mailto:smanettu@gmail.com)**

Please include your app version and a description of the issue. Do not send
passwords, Google sign-in codes or full mailbox exports. Redact receipts and
screenshots before emailing them where possible.

## Remove your data

- Use **Settings → Reset all data** to remove saved items, receipt copies,
  claims and Gmail discovery records from the app. This does not delete your
  original photos, files or Gmail messages.
- To stop Gmail access, use **Disconnect Gmail** on the Gmail discovery screen
  or remove ClaimBack from your [Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
  Disconnecting alone does not delete receipts already saved in ClaimBack.
- Resetting the app does not cancel processing already sent to the server.
  Processing jobs and results normally expire seven days after submission.
  Email the address above for help with a server-side data request; include a
  job ID if you have one, and never include an access token or password.

See the [Privacy Notice](../privacy/) for processing, retention and provider details.
